# Tool activity rendering: prior art

Inspected 2026-10-13 through Librarian, then checked the cited raw first-party source directly. This records prior-art evidence and the tradeoffs behind Rui's accepted rendering decision, not implementation qualification.

The final agreement requires a Bash description in original arguments, unchanged, nonblank, single-line, valid UTF-8, and at most 100 decoded bytes. The initial 120-byte limit is superseded. Base verb, concrete target, and useful scope are style guidance, not prose-quality rejection. There is no backward compatibility. [Architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md#staged-persistent-input) owns the accepted live-region and permanent-outcome contract.

## Finding and accepted choice

There is no single industry rule that tool starts and results must be appended separately. Codex demonstrates mutable trailing activity followed by native-history insertion. Pi demonstrates a retained mutable document, including scrollback clearing and replay. Gemini's behavior depends on its renderer. Claude Code documents native-scrollback and alternate-screen modes, but does not expose the core renderer for inspection.

Rui chose an owned mutable region above the composer, followed by one permanent contextual outcome per call and no permanent start line. This supersedes the initial append-only start/result recommendation. Codex provides the strongest inspected example of mutable trailing activity and separate history insertion, but its grouping and interleaving exceptions are not Rui's contract. The sources establish mechanisms and ownership tradeoffs, not an objective UX ranking.

The live region shows at most three oldest calls, fewer on short terminals, with an exact overflow count and `/status` guidance. Settled siblings keep their observed outcomes until permanent presentation succeeds under unchanged full-group publication and call order. Identity, not description text, correlates calls, including after reopening from saved facts. Rui's `•`, `?`, `✓`, and `!` distinguish unresolved, approval, success, and explained failure or uncertainty. Denial and rejection remain neutral and explicit. These are Rui decisions, not a universal glyph scheme inferred from prior art.

## What "updates the line" means

| Model | What changes | What remains in native scrollback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Append-only permanent output | New start/result records append; earlier records are untouched. | Both records, subject to terminal retention. |
| Mutable trailing content, then commit | Cursor-addressed redraw affects an owned live region. Its settled representation is later inserted into history. | Normally the settled representation, not every animation or intermediate state. |
| Retained viewport redraw | Application history remains a render source, on the main or alternate screen. | Main-screen implementations may clear/replay it; alternate-screen history is not native scrollback. |

Snapshots cannot distinguish these mechanisms. Component mutation alone also cannot prove cursor behavior or history permanence.

### Codex establishes the boundary, including its exception

Codex retires an active **cell/group**, not each completed member separately. `should_flush` requires a non-exploration cell and every member's duration to be present. Ordinary single-command cells therefore flush on completion. Compatible exploration calls join one group, which remains open for adjacent reads, lists, and searches even after all members finish. This is not a universal whole-batch completion barrier. See [completion and grouping](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/exec_cell/model.rs#L92-L165) and [start ownership](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/chatwidget/command_lifecycle.rs#L243-L311).

An incompatible B start can flush still-running A. A's later completion creates a separate history cell while B remains active, rather than updating A's committed entry. Thus "one final permanent row per call" is not universal even in Codex. See [ownership transfer without a completion check](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/chatwidget.rs#L1203-L1208) and [ID-based completion routing](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/chatwidget/command_lifecycle.rs#L392-L450).

The backend distinction is explicit. [Frame drawing](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/custom_terminal.rs#L352-L396) diffs buffers and restores the cursor. [History insertion](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/insert_history.rs#L135-L218) separately uses cursor movement, scroll regions, and CRLF, protecting the composer. Its `FullScreen` insertion strategy is not itself alternate-screen rendering. [Owned-screen history](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/app/history_ui.rs#L54-L108) instead defers native insertion.

Ordinary previews say `Running`, then `Ran`, with an activity marker or a green/red `•`. Success and failure share the bullet shape. [Detailed transcript rendering](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/exec_cell/transcript.rs#L16-L68) uses `$`, output, and `✓` or `✗` with exit code/duration. These are different views, not interchangeable glyph evidence. See [preview state](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/exec_cell/render.rs#L454-L481) and [bounded preview details](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/12cca49c81c8aa6a37a5cc4e39964ee3b70f9458/codex-rs/tui/src/exec_cell/render.rs#L540-L574).

### Pi updates components, not an immutable history prefix

[Execution events](https://github.com/badlogic/pi-mono/blob/7d75a0e3eb273dff753d49c066ea29fbe7b3b6f6/packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/interactive-mode.ts#L3606-L3650) route by call ID. Completion removes the pending-map entry but leaves the chat component. Interleaved updates therefore target distinct components without completion-order reordering. This proves presentation routing, not scheduler concurrency.

[Regular-mode rendering](https://github.com/badlogic/pi-mono/blob/7d75a0e3eb273dff753d49c066ea29fbe7b3b6f6/packages/tui/src/tui-main-screen.ts#L450-L492) uses relative cursor movement and CRLF. An older offscreen change invokes [clear and full replay](https://github.com/badlogic/pi-mono/blob/7d75a0e3eb273dff753d49c066ea29fbe7b3b6f6/packages/tui/src/tui-main-screen.ts#L277-L350), emitting `CSI 2J`, home, and `CSI 3J`. Width and ordinary height changes also take that path. This is not append-only permanence. [Mode selection](https://github.com/badlogic/pi-mono/blob/7d75a0e3eb273dff753d49c066ea29fbe7b3b6f6/packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/tui-renderer.ts#L18-L49) separately chooses regular or alternate-screen rendering.

Pi's [component state](https://github.com/badlogic/pi-mono/blob/7d75a0e3eb273dff753d49c066ea29fbe7b3b6f6/packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/components/tool-execution.ts#L270-L295) selects pending, error, or success backgrounds, not a universal checkmark vocabulary. Bash uses [the `$` prompt](https://github.com/badlogic/pi-mono/blob/7d75a0e3eb273dff753d49c066ea29fbe7b3b6f6/packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/bash.ts#L417-L431), with [the last five visual output lines when collapsed](https://github.com/badlogic/pi-mono/blob/7d75a0e3eb273dff753d49c066ea29fbe7b3b6f6/packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/renderers/bash.ts#L18-L89). Expansion and truncation/full-output notices are separate concerns.

### Gemini separates retirement order from rendering mode

[History promotion](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/51617c1bcbcb038291125b092501b4342f4e7f77/packages/cli/src/ui/hooks/useGeminiStream.ts#L450-L501) stops at the first unfinished call. A later completed sibling remains pending; contiguous agent groups receive additional completion lookahead. [Whole-batch completion](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/51617c1bcbcb038291125b092501b4342f4e7f77/packages/cli/src/ui/hooks/useGeminiStream.ts#L307-L349) adds remaining calls to history and clears live display.

[Legacy composition](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/51617c1bcbcb038291125b092501b4342f4e7f77/packages/cli/src/ui/components/MainContent.tsx#L405-L423) places history in Ink `Static` and pending items afterward. [Viewport composition](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/51617c1bcbcb038291125b092501b4342f4e7f77/packages/cli/src/ui/components/MainContent.tsx#L349-L382) uses `ScrollableList`; only the header is classified static, and comments acknowledge scrollback clearing. [Ink render options](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/51617c1bcbcb038291125b092501b4342f4e7f77/packages/cli/src/interactiveCli.tsx#L138-L178) select terminal/alternate buffers. This verifies application composition, not Ink's dependency-level cursor algorithm or permanent byte-level append-only output.

[State rendering](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/51617c1bcbcb038291125b092501b4342f4e7f77/packages/cli/src/ui/components/messages/ToolShared.tsx#L145-L189) uses a spinner while executing. [Other symbols](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/51617c1bcbcb038291125b092501b4342f4e7f77/packages/cli/src/ui/constants.ts#L19-L27) are `o` pending, `?` confirming, `✓` success, `-` cancelled, and `x` error. The `⊷` constant does not establish the actual running display. [Display mapping](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/51617c1bcbcb038291125b092501b4342f4e7f77/packages/cli/src/ui/hooks/toolMapping.ts#L43-L128) carries name/description, live output/progress, confirmation details, and final result. Error descriptions become serialized arguments, unlike Rui's unchanged-description agreement.

### Claude supports UX claims, not retirement mechanics

The public repository links to docs but does not supply the inspected core renderer. [Fullscreen documentation](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/fullscreen#what-changes) says classic mode retains native scrollback, whereas fullscreen uses an alternate buffer and visible-message rendering. [Mouse behavior](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/fullscreen#use-the-mouse) documents expanding tool call/result together, including live `!` shell progress. [Transcript controls](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode#general-controls) expose detailed execution and expand grouped MCP lines such as `Called slack 3 times`. [Background Bash](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode#background-bash-commands) returns task IDs while work continues; these docs do not establish concurrent row ordering.

[Transcript export](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/fullscreen#search-and-review-the-conversation) can write the expanded conversation into native scrollback. This explicit export is not proof of per-tool append behavior during execution. The pinned [changelog](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/blob/addd7a69690bb5f5484c2a47583a8f88c4842a6e/CHANGELOG.md#L55-L61) distinguishes dim `Not run` from a red error. It does not establish a complete glyph state machine.

## Fit and limits for Rui

At initial inspection, [SessionTerminal](../src/SessionTerminal.zig) owned the redrawable spacer, status, and draft. `permanent` cleared that region and wrote transcript bytes. [SessionFrontend](../src/SessionFrontend.zig) suppressed redraw from first transcript bytes through joined completion. [Activity rendering](../src/session_view.zig) emitted permanent result summaries but drained call fields without displaying a start label. These are historical observations of the pre-change renderer, not qualification of the implementation now in progress.

The accepted mutable region requires bounded display ownership and exact call identity. Reopening reconstructs activity from saved facts, not volatile rows. Ordered full-group publication preserves call order but delays permanent outcomes behind an unfinished sibling; the live region retains observed settlement during that delay. Shared Store-derived `ToolPresentation` on Current, Activity, and Conversation supplies labels from original arguments and outcomes from canonical facts, without a presentation table, copied log, or description map. Malformed descriptors have nullable labels. Exact command and Workspace consent, warnings, uncertainty, `/details`, and draft/cursor preservation remain mandatory.

No source establishes an objective best UX, terminal portability, Rui resource costs, or Rui's glyph scheme. The original investigation was source-only, with no installs, providers, runtime/emulator checks, or screenshots. GitHub commit identities and citation ranges were checked; hosted docs cannot be pinned to the repository commits. Accepted decisions are now recorded in Architecture, while native platform and visual evidence remain required by [Verification](../VERIFICATION.md#inspection-and-waits).
