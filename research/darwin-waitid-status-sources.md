## Status representations and the immediate correction

- [POSIX Issue 8 `<signal.h>`, SIGCHLD `si_status`](https://pubs.opengroup.org/onlinepubs/9799919799/basedefs/signal.h.html) explicitly requires **the full exit value** for `CLD_EXITED`, not just the least significant eight bits; otherwise `si_status` is the signal causing the change. [POSIX `wait`/`waitpid`](https://pubs.opengroup.org/onlinepubs/9799919799/functions/wait.html) defines `WEXITSTATUS` as the **low-order eight bits** of the `_exit` argument. These are different observations, not interchangeable status integers. This edition's full-value requirement should not be retroactively attributed to older POSIX editions or used to infer the exact kernel's entire integer-width behavior.
- XNU [`kern_exit.c:838-841`](https://github.com/apple/darwin-xnu/blob/2ff845c2e033bd0ff64b5b6aa6063a1f8f65aa32/bsd/kern/kern_exit.c#L838-L841) records the top exit byte separately and encodes the exit argument into `p_xstat`; [`:2279-2302`](https://github.com/apple/darwin-xnu/blob/2ff845c2e033bd0ff64b5b6aa6063a1f8f65aa32/bsd/kern/kern_exit.c#L2279-L2302) fills `waitid` `CLD_EXITED` `si_status` from the exit status and separately ORs the saved top byte. By contrast [`wait4` masks `p_xstat` with `0xffff`](https://github.com/apple/darwin-xnu/blob/2ff845c2e033bd0ff64b5b6aa6063a1f8f65aa32/bsd/kern/kern_exit.c#L2008-L2021), with the explicit comment “Legacy apps expect only 8 bits of status”; [`wait.h:143-147`](https://github.com/apple/darwin-xnu/blob/2ff845c2e033bd0ff64b5b6aa6063a1f8f65aa32/bsd/sys/wait.h#L143-L147) has both masked UNIX03 and unmasked compatibility `WEXITSTATUS` definitions. The probe establishes the actual returned values on its machine; source documents the separate paths, but build-conditional details prevent deriving that machine's exact full-width behavior from `main` alone.
- Rui's [`rui_bash_observe` copies `info.si_status` without normalization](https://github.com/DivyanshGolyan/rui/blob/a7e18934a1ff4e9df87f1a840303f949871edf70/src/bash_platform.c#L16-L43), while [`rui_bash_reap` uses `WEXITSTATUS`](https://github.com/DivyanshGolyan/rui/blob/a7e18934a1ff4e9df87f1a840303f949871edf70/src/bash_platform.c#L45-L71). Zig [`decodeObservation` casts both to `u8`](https://github.com/DivyanshGolyan/rui/blob/a7e18934a1ff4e9df87f1a840303f949871edf70/src/bash.zig#L155-L161), then [`reapLeader` compares](https://github.com/DivyanshGolyan/rui/blob/a7e18934a1ff4e9df87f1a840303f949871edf70/src/bash.zig#L759-L784). **Proven mismatch:** the native probe's out-of-u8 values trap under the current checked cast, before comparison. Normalize *only `CLD_EXITED`* to the low eight bits in the C observation adapter, preserving signal numbers and unexpected codes unchanged. Native regression: direct children `_exit(257)`, `_exit(512)`, `_exit(-1)`, plus signal termination; assert observed/final-reaped kind and value independently and owner release. This proves the adapter, not the unknown live child argument.
