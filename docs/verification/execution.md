# OnePage execution verification

This is part of the normative [VERIFICATION.md](../../VERIFICATION.md) contract. [Corresponding architecture](../architecture/execution.md) supplies the governing rules.

## Operations, Attempts, and recovery

Attempt is a physical-try concept whose current admission is recorded on its Operation. Resolution is an immutable value owned directly by that Operation; neither is a separate result identity or historical per-try ledger. Fixtures exercise the real Store and adapter interfaces against the following boundaries.

| Boundary | Required fresh-process result |
| --- | --- |
| Before Attempt admission commit | No new execution identity, consumed allowance or external dispatch authority survives rollback. |
| After Attempt admission commit, before dispatch | Exact current identity and consumed allowance survive. Recovery follows conservative effect-specific uncertainty and never recreates the old Dispatch Permit. |
| During provider/tool execution | Current admission is discoverable without a SQLite transaction spanning execution, payload-sized resident buffer, reducer image or notification history. |
| After scratch seal, before settlement | Scratch and Execution Evidence are non-authoritative; process death loses them and leaves the prior admitted uncertainty for recovery. |
| Final settlement | Required immutable content/evidence, the Operation's previously absent Resolution value, Conversation or permission facts and semantic consequences commit together or not at all. The Operation ID identifies the result thereafter. |
| Retryable model settlement | Current failure classification, eligibility and required policy accounting commit together while Resolution remains absent. No permanent Completion is created and waiting work owns no resident worker or timer. |
| Replacement admission | Atomically revalidate unresolved/applicable work, due time and allowance; replace waiting eligibility with a fresh admitted identity; preserve all consumed policy facts. A stale due selection cannot launch a second replacement. |
| Interrupted settlement | Direct Model Interruption or an applicable Session stop commits an immutable Interrupted Resolution value with exact causal provenance, with or without an admitted try or terminal evidence. It fences retry/acceptance; custody separately suppresses launch or drains transport and retains capacity until safe release. |
| After Resolution, before observation | Reads and public keyed/domain replay recover immutable meaning through the Operation ID without another effect, result identity or historical internal evidence replay. |

Fixtures distinguish never-admitted work, admitted uncertainty and retry waiting without a redundant cached phase. Attempt identities/ordinals cannot wrap or be reused to match stale callbacks. Final meaning and its required content/provenance are immutable: reject replacement, clearing, a second accepted output set or accepting late evidence after interruption. Permission denial, validation failure, interruption and cancellation must resolve work without fabricated execution evidence. An Operation-derived failed Turn Outcome references a resolved Operation in that same Turn through its Operation ID.

Trace failed A, admitted B and crash at every transaction boundary. Frozen inputs, consumed allowance, current retry eligibility, unresolved-effect uncertainty, exact authorization and accepted continuation must remain recoverable with diagnostic history deleted. Retry accounting belongs to each model Operation; replacement and restart cannot reset its consumed tries. Ordinary new model Operations have independent retry allowance and no aggregate Turn counter. The selected accounting has one authority and must not require historical Attempt rows. Cover arithmetic exhaustion and rollback without lost charges or leaked dispatch authority; exact numeric policy remains with its limit owner.

Model recovery records possible duplicate work/billing and uses the same manifest for policy-authorized new Attempts. Bash is never automatically replayed. Edit retains exact authorized intent and observes preimage, expected postimage, divergence and invalid target; after custody loss even matching postimage does not prove OnePage produced it. Preserve indeterminate meaning with the observation under ADR-0004. No diagnostic deletion can erase that final evidence.

Exercise terminal delivery, cancellation and detachment races through the actual effect-owner interface: at most one evidence delivery escapes, none escapes after detachment, and stale events cannot touch newer custody or replace accepted meaning. Retry waiting becomes inapplicable when the Operation resolves; every admission path revalidates it atomically. Logical Resolution does not release physical resources still draining. Complete sibling Tool Results remain independently committed and projected in call order. Keep the [shared-Session cancellation and public replay fixtures](workflows.md); no historical Completion payload comparison is required.

Streaming fixtures prove that the I/O Reactor writes provider and Bash bytes directly to dynamically charged, immediately unlinked scratch through fixed borrowed windows. No per-Attempt candidate, parser, request, response, or output buffer scales with Active Capacity. Complete model output is parsed once after terminal seal in the shared serial validation/import workspace. Post-commit request-materialization failure becomes evidence for the admitted Attempt.

Post-seal parser fixtures vary payload length and output-item count independently. Verify the shared serial import uses one sequential metadata file, without a per-item file collection or resident item list, and releases it after success or failure. Charge all metadata growth and pending reservations to shared scratch; no separate metadata-file quota is required. Prove bounded resident parser/validation state, scratch-backed range metadata when cardinality grows, exact preservation of required provider bytes, and incremental import after complete provider validation. Cover fragmented and escaped input, opaque continuation and unknown fields under the adapter contract, a malformed final item, scratch exhaustion, and rollback after an initial content write. No partial semantic success, incomplete content reference, or recoverable range index may escape. Exercise the real adapter and settlement transaction: syntactic JSON validity and the narrow experimental validator do not establish full provider or output-schema correctness. Preserve the existing effect/recovery fixtures and add cases at actual missing boundaries rather than a parallel recovery framework.

Storage failure injection covers full, I/O, allocation, corrupt content, foreign reference, wrong digest, and transaction rollback. SQLite faults expose neither half a semantic relation nor content without its first reference.

## Bash timeout policy

Verify an omitted `timeout` selects five minutes and an explicit value replaces that default in the admitted Action. Permission/capacity waiting must not consume it, and continuous output must not reset it. Test expiry through the existing effect owner: preserve available evidence, publish a typed timeout Tool Result, retain custody through cleanup, and never automatically replay uncertain Bash. Default changes after Action admission cannot change its selected timeout. Verify a representable override longer than five minutes is accepted without an arbitrary ceiling; reject nonpositive, nonfinite or unrepresentable values and deadline arithmetic overflow without clamping. The historical two-minute source maximum is not the accepted default; production implementation remains pending.

## Model retry and inactivity policy

Exercise temporary connection failures, rate limits, temporary server failures and inactivity timeouts against the shared per-Operation retry allowance. Verify invalid requests, unsupported responses and authentication failures that refresh cannot fix do not trigger blind retries. Provider-specific fixtures must distinguish temporary from permanent errors and route confirmed context overflow through compaction with a new manifest, not an equal-manifest retry. Storage failure and deterministic continuation failure remain non-retryable through this policy.

Verify startup configuration can change retry count/waits, provider inactivity, the tool-output excerpt size and the default Bash timeout. Exercise non-default values through their owning boundaries, including a per-call Bash timeout overriding the configured default. Restart must not reset consumed tries or saved retry eligibility, alter an admitted Action's selected timeout, or rewrite an existing Tool Result. Configuration spelling is implementation work; live reload is not required.

Using deterministic transport and clock fixtures, verify one initial model Attempt plus at most three retries, default waits of 2/4/8 seconds, a longer valid Retry-After taking precedence, and a shorter one not shortening the default. Restart and admission rollback must preserve the correct consumed count and next retry time. Cover uncertain disconnect with possible duplicate billing, timeout exhaustion, and the existing safe failure settlement without skipping tool/effect obligations.

Verify five minutes of silence before the first response-body chunk and between chunks triggers timeout. Body data and heartbeat chunks reset the timer; a stream making progress for more than five minutes must continue. Distinguish provider waiting from Host admission and local request preparation. Local timeout does not establish remote cancellation. The synthetic Python timing probe is preliminary policy evidence, not a passing production timeout test or qualification of Claude.

## Native Edit verification

Verify Edit does not inherit the Bash execution timeout or provider-inactivity timeout and introduces no separate execution/reconciliation timer. Use deterministic time advancement during chunked work to show elapsed time alone does not fail it. Cancellation before mutation must leave the target unchanged; cancellation after mutation begins must retain safe completion/reconciliation and custody until settlement. Slow/erroring filesystem scenarios must still exercise the existing service and recovery requirements.

Exercise the actual module interface: empty/absent/ambiguous/identical search, overlapping occurrences, matches spanning every read boundary, deletion by empty replacement, UTF-8, BOM and CRLF preservation, and long source/replacement/search inputs. Verify rejection before live mutation for invalid exact edits. Exercise tracked, untracked and ignored existing files, files outside any Git repository, relative paths and absolute targets outside the Session Workspace. Prove Git tracking and Workspace containment do not gate eligibility, while filesystem access failures, exact target/preimage validation and ordinary Action authorization remain enforced. Ordinary path resolution, including symlinks, must not retarget authorized work unnoticed. Compare every successful output with an independent byte reference. No fuzzy fallback, whole-file normalization or implicit replace-all may occur.

Verify bounded argument decoding, streaming source/replacement I/O, reusable 16 KiB copy workspace, search-text/overlap high-water and release between phases. Test decoded search text at exactly 16,384 bytes and rejection at 16,385 bytes before Authorization or mutation, including multibyte UTF-8 and escaped wire input. Verify over-limit decoding does not first materialize the entire search string. Vary file size and line count independently; neither should create file/line-proportional resident preparation state. Count concurrent workspaces under actual ownership; standalone executable peaks are not per-edit allocations inside the Host. Do not claim borrowed memory is free.

Inspect and exercise the three Edit scratch file roles across preparation, copying, handoff and cleanup to verify the derived file population and ownership. This checks implementation structure and leaks, not a separate file-count quota; no fourth-file rejection mechanism is required. Charge source/output overlap and materialized input to the shared scratch allowance; include empty files in the count. Verify early release and no per-field or per-phase file accumulation.

Verify preparation, execution and cleanup together never exceed shared Active Capacity. At saturation, pending preparation holds no per-waiter resources and creates no Attempt or Dispatch Permit. Permission waits release scratch, buffers and custody after intent persistence; approval reacquires capacity and revalidates the exact authorized intent. Cover cancellation, failed intent persistence, crash/restart and stale preparation events without inventing mutation evidence.

Trace each preparation/apply/reconciliation phase through service yields. Large files, repeated-prefix search inputs and slow/erroring file I/O must preserve the selected control-service contract; the Host control path cannot hide one unbounded synchronous editor call. Preserve per-file ownership across suspension, reject stale execution identities and prove no buffer is used simultaneously by incompatible borrowers. Qualify the supported string-search implementation rather than assuming all libc implementations have identical worst-case behavior.

Inject failures before/after intent persistence and authorization, during streamed output creation, at live-target revalidation, during partial mutation and before result commit. Retain existing permission, exact target/preimage/postimage, storage-failure, cancellation and effect-uncertainty fixtures. Show that an Edit failure cannot publish partial success or authorize blind replay, and that Host crash restarts from durable facts. In-process placement offers no independent native-crash isolation. Verify the module has no SQLite/provider-credential access and that Host orchestration does not depend on its matching/copy implementation.

The local exact-edit prototype at `codex/exact-edit-probe` commits `7c72bdb`, `ff24807` and `63581a1` demonstrates preparation feasibility, baseline attribution and buffer reuse. Its 36 variant correctness cases and 45 benchmark cases do not implement live mutation, authorized recovery, bounded service turns or production argument decoding. Git helper evidence is historical following ADR-0027; any remaining Git-based eligibility check still counts as OnePage overhead until explicitly replaced or removed.
