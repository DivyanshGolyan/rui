# OnePage resource verification

This is part of the normative [VERIFICATION.md](../../VERIFICATION.md) contract. [Corresponding architecture](../architecture/resources.md) supplies the governing rules.

## Memory and density

### Capacity and control service

The [accepted execution-control simplification](../design/execution-control-simplicity.md) also requires fixed-table saturation and reuse checks: free plus occupied equals configured capacity, reservation rollback restores capacity, commit-before-launch crash does not recreate a Dispatch Permit, and cancellation retains live resource custody until safe release. Delayed/duplicate events cannot affect a reused record's newer Attempt. Benchmark plain scans and completion bursts at the selected capacities before considering another index; exploratory capacity 1,000 is not an accepted product ceiling. Measure actual idle CPU and wake counts separately from per-scan arithmetic, preserving the required retry poll and deadlines.

At full Active Capacity, make additional accepted work eligible and verify that it remains in existing SQLite facts without a new Attempt, consumed Attempt allowance, Dispatch Permit, request scratch, or per-waiter resident state. Verify no busy driving or new capacity-poll timer while waiting, control progress without a free execution credit, and bounded reconsideration after safe custody release. Cancel before release and prove the cancelled work cannot launch; race cancellation with admission under the existing transaction/launch rules. Restart with waiting work and prove eligibility survives without inventing a prior execution; keep previously admitted uncertainty on its existing recovery path. Verify independent ingress/storage limits still apply. Increase the durable waiting population separately from active capacity and measure memory and query-work slopes rather than introducing a memory-motivated backlog quota.

During ordinary completion bursts, submit stop/cancel through the real command boundary and measure arrival, durable acknowledgement, reactor interruption and full physical-custody/scratch release separately. Verify service between individual result settlements for both ready controls and already-sealed cancellation cleanup, while preserving atomic imports and progress for ordinary work and inspection under sustained ready controls. Include an in-progress long import, completion/cancellation races and the selected saturated capacity. Verify cancellation arriving during a busy network batch, service between socket events and completed-transfer notifications, and safe rejection of stale readiness after handle removal or descriptor reuse. Measure individual socket/timeout actions and callbacks on the supported transport build, including mixed network and subprocess readiness. Fast acknowledgement alone is not a passing cancellation-latency result; the synthetic sender and deliberately nonfinishing target used in the prototype do not certify these production boundaries.

### Temporary storage and retention

Verify the configurable 8 GiB default aggregate scratch allowance using logical-file-byte accounting across simultaneous request materialization, output/metadata, ingress, current and retained reports, temporary exports, and retained Spillover Output. Exercise the same accounting with a smaller test allowance to force boundaries without requiring multi-gigabyte fixtures. No allowance-sized RAM/disk preallocation is permitted. Verify prompt charge/file release when there is no later consumer, and transfer to shared temporary-file ownership for optional retained output without keeping an execution slot. Protect outstanding Host-owned I/O; canonical state and persistent diagnostics remain separate, while their temporary copies count. Include real volume exhaustion independently of the configured allowance.

Verify shared FIFO reclamation under a small test allowance. Mix protected request/capture/Edit/report/Host-read files with completed disposable spillover; reclaim only the oldest eligible files when space is needed, preserving accounting through ownership transfer and safe deletion. Preserve saved result publication before spillover becomes disposable, skip files protected by Host-owned work, and exercise failed deletion or an entirely protected population without early charge refunds, quota oversubscription or busy retries. Normal Turn completion does not delete spillover by a separate rule; later Turns can retrieve it while retained. FIFO removal and Host exit/crash leave the durable small result unchanged and produce explicit unavailability without automatic old-command replay. Include retained-file metadata/handle populations in memory and descriptor accounting; active-execution file subtotals alone do not bound this new retained population.

Exercise published spillover opened by Bash and inherited by a descendant across shell exit. FIFO may remove its name and return its Host retention charge after Host closure even while external handles keep physical storage alive. Verify this does not block FIFO, retarget the saved result or cause automatic old-command replay; label physical storage above the retention allowance as excluded external usage rather than falsely claiming an all-process cap. External writes, links, truncation and replacement do not bypass private Host reservations because they are outside the published-spillover accounting contract. No sandbox, inferred reader tracking or external-mutation polling is required. Preserve explicit real-disk-full failure.

For private scratch and active capture, verify growth reservation before I/O, overflow rejection, sparse-gap charging, no additional charge for in-place overwrites, unused-reservation return after partial writes/errors, and charge return only after successful truncation or final safe closure. Race distinct file owners against one cap and prove outstanding reservations cannot oversubscribe; after Host quiescence, the total equals owned private logical sizes plus recorded retained-spillover charges. Transfer ownership without dropping/repeating charges; count simultaneous copies separately. For private scratch and active capture, initial unlink, failed truncation and pending I/O must not free capacity prematurely. Published spillover name removal follows the explicit external-reader exception above. Cover every file-growth path and writable-handle lifetime, not only the prototype's pwrite wrapper. Verify no per-write content allocation, directory scan or SQLite transaction; measure accounting alongside real Host I/O without inferring exact overhead from noisy prototype timings.

### Storage failures

Inject temporary-storage exhaustion before Attempt admission, after admission during request materialization, during streamed output, and during post-seal processing. Before admission, prove waiting consumes no new Attempt allowance or per-waiter execution resources. After admission, prove the real identity/accounting is preserved, the affected execution follows its effect-specific stop/cleanup path, no automatic redispatch occurs, and partial output cannot become semantic success or continuation. Verify that execution does not retain scratch indefinitely while paused for more space, and that failure handling does not grow a RAM backlog. Include Bash with an already-applied external change and Edit requiring reconciliation; preserve uncertainty rather than claiming rollback.

Inject a canonical SQLite storage fault that prevents commit, including when scratch and SQLite share a full volume. Verify dispatch stops, bounded effect-aware Host shutdown occurs, and no durable outcome or occupancy release is falsely reported. Restore storage and explicitly restart; verify previously committed results and effect-specific recovery without fabricated terminal facts or a recreated Dispatch Permit. Test diagnostic-write failure separately: it must not trigger this canonical-storage shutdown policy by itself. Existing ingress and inspection checks must still reject incomplete results. These tests select no guaranteed free-space reserve or numeric disk quota.

### Memory measurement and populations

Across repeated work-to-idle cycles, distinguish allocator-live bytes and blocks, allocator reservation, process physical footprint and resource counts. Observe ordinary cleanup first; separate diagnostic library closure, natural idle and any explicit allocator-relief probe. A footprint reduction with unchanged live allocation volume is evidence of reclaimability, not proof of leak freedom, exact per-library attribution or a required runtime reclamation policy. Account for unsupported allocator/SQLite counters and direct VM/kernel/file-cache costs explicitly.

Measurements report whole-process RSS and each independent axis:

- Dormant Session count;
- terminal and nonterminal Turn count;
- Active Capacity and occupied Active Credits;
- Storage Owner, I/O Reactor, and temporary Action-executor incremental and retained-idle cost;
- the one content-free Physical Custody table's record size and occupancy, where each occupied record is one Active Credit;
- transport-library threads, stacks, fixed windows, sockets, and resolver resources;
- subprocess trees and model-requested workload memory;
- shared serial validation/import workspace high-water;
- SQLite heap, database bytes, journal bytes, and writes;
- Workflow Evaluator heap, bridge memory, process RSS, and replay count;
- immutable content, unlinked-scratch logical and physical bytes, filesystem-cache pressure, and raw-plus-canonical overlap.

SQLite memory evidence records the pinned version/build, requested and effective cache size, effective spill threshold in pages, page size, heap-limit readback and enforcement prerequisites, and allocation/page-cache high-water and spill counts across incremental imports of increasing size. Include a diagnostic spill-disabled control that exposes dirty-page growth and a supported-spill case that completes larger-than-cache imports under the selected heap budget. Test allocation failure and rollback through the actual Store boundary without publishing partial content or semantic success. Distinguish SQLite-accounted memory from whole-process and filesystem-cache costs; the experimental heap/cache values are not production defaults. Publication of a passing pinned-build prototype does not certify the integrated importer.

Verify that Store initialization and admission do not retain an artificial canonical-database page-count quota or emergency reserved-page threshold. Check the effective supported SQLite representation boundaries separately from memory settings. Actual full-volume and failed-commit injection must still exercise the canonical-storage shutdown and recovery rules; removing a quota cannot turn an unsuccessful write into committed success.

Derive the internal finite SQLite heap ceiling, cache sizes and transfer windows during implementation, and qualify the chosen values against the existing 256 MiB whole-Host target with representative imports, queries and mixed work. Verify actual heap-limit enforcement and explicit exhaustion behavior; do not treat the historical 8 MiB allowance or a passing small prototype as the selected value. Numeric tuning does not gate the prior design decision, but integrated qualification is required before release. A restriction of promised behavior or a missed Host target requires explicit review.

Record effective journal/synchronous, page, mmap, temporary-storage, busy and feature-hardening settings on the pinned build. Prepare and execute every supported statement/schema shape under any retained SQL limits, and verify content representation separately from SQL text or row limits. Demonstrate that the compiled-program ceiling is not used as an execution-step or time budget; distinguish a preparation-limit `SQLITE_NOMEM` from an injected allocation failure in diagnostics. Exercise immediate busy reporting, failed preparation, failed read-only capture, admission rollback, failed settlement/commit and unusable rollback state at their respective semantic boundaries. No failed path may acknowledge an unsaved stable result, publish partial content, release unsaved occupancy or automatically replay a mutation. Preserve the existing canonical-fault shutdown/restart and effect-recovery requirements.

Required population points are 0, 100, 1,000, and 10,000 Dormant Sessions at fixed capacity, then Active Capacity 1, 10, 50, 100, and the planned default 1,000 at fixed durable population. Repeated 0→capacity→0 churn must return resident orchestration memory to the same bounded envelope. Terminal Turn population adds durable bytes rather than resident execution objects. At each active point, report end-to-end Turn latency, provider and tool overhead, evaluator replay, SQLite transaction cost, and throughput alongside memory and OS-resource measurements.

Qualify the planned 1,000 shared Active Capacity with model-heavy, Bash-heavy, Edit-heavy and mixed work, including OnePage-owned temporary workers/helpers and separately observed model-selected workload processes. Record actual stack use/reservation, process and kernel resources, descriptors, scratch, CPU/contention, controls and full cleanup. The artificial 1,000-worker fixture chose its own stack load and is not evidence of actual OnePage executor cost or topology. Set targets from intended ownership and representative execution, then verify the real Host. A failure requires attribution and an explicit budget/capacity or implementation decision before release, not silently excluding OnePage-owned helper memory.

Every new allocation topology records its owner, multiplier, maximum, ordinary occupancy, release boundary, failure behaviour, and reason an existing owner cannot serve it before implementation.

Certification additionally measures unopened, Store-open, and Host-started idle baselines, with Active Capacity zero where supported, and separate idle CLI cost. Connected-client population varies independently from external Active Capacity and durable Run count, including slow inbound/outbound transfers, saturation, and connection churn. Distinguish analytical worst case, reserved virtual memory, physical resident/private dirty memory, observed high-water, kernel/socket resources, file descriptors, filesystem cache/writeback, and temporary disk. Use representative Conversation/content in the 10,000-Session fixture and check in raw inputs and calculations. Numeric pass/fail targets come from the approved limit matrix; any unexplained slope or budget miss triggers architecture review before another pool or cache is added.

Verify model request/response and Bash input/stdout/stderr file roles stream through bounded windows, charge overlapping lifetimes to shared scratch, and release after safe completion/import. Exercise repeated executions and error cleanup to detect accumulated files or handles. File counts are derived from these roles, not enforced by per-tool quota checks.

Verify local upload and report/export scratch share the aggregate byte allowance, including overlapping incoming/outgoing files. Exercise success, failure, disconnect and ownership handoff: release files after last use and safe I/O completion, preserve charges across handoff, and retain content still needed by committed work. Reports and exports reuse the outgoing role without a detached file backlog or separate quota.

Verify at most one evaluation lifecycle per Host, including outcome import/failure handling and safe child/pipe cleanup before reuse. Queue bursts while model/tool I/O and controls remain active; measure queue delay separately from evaluation service time. Include 1,000 tiny evaluations and a runaway followed by ordinary work without retaining per-waiter evaluator state or detached captures. The serial prototype is supporting evidence, not integrated control-latency qualification.

Exercise the 1-second evaluator process CPU budget and 5-second parent-owned lifetime deadline from successful spawn through complete output and exit. Include ordinary replay, native decoding/encoding, excessive promise jobs, CPU loops, a non-cooperative or stopped child, truncated output, and a child that closes output but does not exit. Require explicit failure and no partial publication, actual child reap/pipe closure before the next evaluation, and truthful cause classification. Waiting before spawn and model/tool execution do not consume these deadlines. Record OS scheduling overshoot separately; do not promise instantaneous termination or classify arbitrary signals as memory exhaustion. Derive and verify the kernel backstop and cooperative checks rather than treating historical constants as passing evidence.

Qualify the initial 16 MiB engine-managed heap limit with representative full workflow replay, large-report serialization and canonical publication, including workloads at the byte and object-count scales observed in saved workflows. Exercise allocation exhaustion without partial success and verify safe evaluator cleanup. Report engine allocation limits separately from native allocations and whole-process physical footprint; small-summary assembly probes are supporting evidence, not end-to-end qualification.

Qualify replacements for historical source/argument/visible-result byte caps, total-entry and request-count limits with workflows exceeding each old boundary while fitting actual memory/work/scratch budgets. Test byte length, item count, request count and nesting independently. Preserve exact repeated-key binding, complete pending sets and deterministic replay; unsupported shapes must not pass by dropping data. Confirm native indexing/allocation and traversal remain bounded before removing former guards. The finite 10,000-await and endless-await fixtures must demonstrate that CPU/lifetime checks replace the independent microtask quota without accepting runaway work.

Verify explicit workflow keys across Session creation, configuration and messages: same-key equal-input replay returns the original result, changed bindings conflict, and distinct keys preserve intentional identical operations. Cover loop iterations, reused helpers and nested parallel branches whose invocation order changes when cached results become available, including the [A, B to A, C, B counterexample](../../research/workflow-call-order/README.md). Require integrated admission/result binding checks; the historical evaluator fixture alone does not prove Host recovery. Check exact caller-key text, including non-ASCII, case distinctions and canonically equivalent but differently encoded Unicode, without normalization or arbitrary field caps. Exercise actual exchange/resource exhaustion with explicit rejection and no truncation or partial admission.

On the supported Mac build, verify there is no fixed 64 MiB address-space setter or claimed whole-process memory ceiling derived from it. Retain explicit engine/native allocation enforcement and whole-Host physical-footprint measurements. The required Linux target must separately establish enforcement and compatibility evidence for its selected guards; successful limit readback alone is not proof of memory protection.

Verify native evaluator allocations remain bounded outside the QuickJS heap and that phase-local buffer reuse leaves no live references to overwritten data. Cover object-key validation before request encoding, duplicate-key rejection, retained canonical request identity, large request sets and explicit allocation exhaustion without partial publication. Report native allocator usage separately from reserved capacity and measured process physical footprint; a reused buffer does not by itself prove an equal RSS reduction. Derive the native capacity as an internal implementation choice and qualify it against supported workflow shapes and aggregate OnePage footprint; review any resulting restriction on promised workflow behavior.

Verify Workflow Output larger than the historical 64 KiB result and 512 KiB frame capacities succeeds when the selected memory/time/scratch resources permit. Exercise the real serializer, parent capture and canonical publication with bounded windows; vary result length independently of item count and nesting. Count temporary conversion allocations at their actual engine/native owner and preserve strict-data checks. Inject memory/time/scratch failure and late validation/import failure; publish no partial success or truncated output. Verify descriptor preparation has its own derived workspace after separating the historical shared constant, and retain representability checks without whole-output allocations. Prototype rejection tests are not successful large-output evidence.

## Accepted Host qualification targets

Before enabling a supported startup configuration, derive complete compiled owner/workspace and per-process descriptor requirements with checked arithmetic and actual OS/library limits. Include transient spawn handles, the single evaluator lifecycle's read-only input and parent capture/publication overlap, and retained spillover metadata/handles after execution custody is released. The active execution/client/import container subtotal is not a complete Host total. Vary retained-file count independently of active execution and bytes, including empty-file cases, and verify bounded resident traversal, reclamation and cleanup. A policy decision closing does not satisfy these implementation checks or whole-Host qualification.

The following are engineering pass/fail targets for named reference fixtures, not runtime admission counters or demonstrated production guarantees. Record the reference laptop, pinned build, fixture inputs and measurement intervals.

- Aggregate OnePage-owned process physical footprint at most **256 MiB**, including its evaluator and helpers, in model-heavy, Bash-heavy, Edit-heavy and mixed qualification workloads at the planned 1,000-operation population. Apply the same bound to cold and retained idle, record both separately and require stable retained memory across repeated churn and no resident growth solely with dormant/history counts. Do not derive component or worker allocations from the artificial worker probe. A necessary-cost miss requires explicit target/default or implementation review before release, not silently excluding helpers.
- Idle CPU below **1% of one core**, including the retry poll. For the model reference fixture of 1,000 streams at 100 small SSE events per second per stream, a short request and bounded terminal burst, average CPU is at most **two cores**, excluding the fixture server. Report tool/mixed CPU separately rather than treating variable useful filesystem work as a universal CPU admission limit.
- **p95 durable control acknowledgement within one second** under defined saturated qualification, including inspection/capture contention. Record maximum observed delay and physical cleanup separately. Individual captures/imports and library calls remain non-preemptible. A miss requires coordination with the query-work owner, not exclusion of capture time; this is not a hard real-time, tool-termination or provider-billing cutoff guarantee.
- Retry eligibility discovery within **two seconds of due time** with free capacity and otherwise light load. Verify one indexed bounded poll per second, no per-Operation timers or catch-up backlog after sleep, and eligibility revalidation at admission. Separate discovery lateness from admission and effect launch; full capacity does not promise immediate dispatch.

Measure process RSS, physical footprint, kernel/socket resources and filesystem cache separately; label aggregate process measurements that can double-count shared mappings. Model-selected Bash processes/descendants remain separately observed workload; helpers OnePage uses to implement Edit remain included. No RSS-triggered controller, allocator reclamation policy or guessed cold/idle partition follows from these targets.

## Limit replacement verification

The [V1 limit matrix](../architecture/resources.md#v1-limit-matrix) is the resource design contract. Implementation must replace each owning representation, prove bounded behavior and explicit failure, then remove its obsolete constants, associated validation and quota-specific tests together. This is a dependency order, not a speculative ticket sequence. Never delete a guard while its fixed buffer/table still needs it; retain strict semantic checks and checked representability throughout. No compatibility layer for superseded unreleased V1 formats is required.
