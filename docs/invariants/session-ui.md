## Seams the harness must provide

- `src/TerminalApp.zig`, `src/Overview.zig`: injected clock and deterministic terminal output/credit, with a pure screen/event harness; a real isolated terminal remains necessary for restoration.
- `src/SignIn.zig`, `src/sign_in.zig`: fake login/launch workers, publication and join barriers, receipt writer and filesystem faults.
- `src/SessionFrontend.zig`, `src/feed_reads.zig`: controllable page/admission completion and cancellation, plus capture and delivery pause points.
- `src/HostDiagnostics.zig`, `src/failed_responses.zig`, `src/stderr.zig`: injected bounded I/O, failure points and clock; temporary private Store and fake provider for Host integration.
- `src/attention.zig`: injectable short-write/flush sink to model acknowledged terminal status delivery.
