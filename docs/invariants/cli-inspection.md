## Seams the harness must provide

- Scripted clocks, observation and signal sources for `src/client.zig` request wait and transport deadlines.
- Injectable capture/dispatch and output sinks for CLI parser, recovery and list rendering in `src/cli.zig` and `src/client.zig`.
- Host ingress, capture and reply pause/failure points plus local in-process wire transport in `src/server.zig`.
- Injected child, readiness and socket transport for `clients/ts/src/client.ts` and `clients/ts/src/transport.ts`.
