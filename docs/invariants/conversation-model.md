## Seams the harness must provide

Proposed harness work, subject to confirming existing injection coverage:

- `src/store/domain/model_admission.zig:804` and `src/store/domain/model_settlement.zig`: deterministic gates before selection/commit and settlement, plus rollback injection and a scheduler drain barrier, exposed through an in-process Host harness.
- `src/model_timing.zig:43` and `src/store/domain/model_admission.zig:1303`: injected clock for deadlines, retries and modeled dates; advance explicitly.
- `src/model_adapter.zig:795` and provider transport: scripted in-memory provider with captured requests, independently gated response chunks, errors and credentials; no network or shared port.
- `src/session_skills.zig` and `src/skills.zig`: injected temporary roots and file-read failures; no process-global home or shared filesystem.
- `src/output_decode.zig` and `src/model_adapter.zig`: public functional decode/encode entry points over byte streams and immutable state where current entry points require Host machinery.
