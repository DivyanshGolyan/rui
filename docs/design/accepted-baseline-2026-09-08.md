This publication reconciles the accepted contract against the live [V1 readiness index](https://github.com/DivyanshGolyan/onepage/issues/2), closed decision maps, their resolutions, and the locally prepared owning amendments. It publishes design, not a runtime implementation or release qualification. The normative owners are [PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md), [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md), [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md), accepted ADRs as amended, [VERIFICATION.md](../../VERIFICATION.md), and [docs/style.md](../style.md).
