# TUI design principles

1. Feedback appears where you acted.
2. The screen shows state, never instructions. A hint is only for a key you could not guess.
3. Every word earns its cell: no label for the obvious, no value at its normal level, no fact twice on one screen.
4. Nothing moves unless something happened. Panels open instantly; the only motion is work in progress.
5. Tint is what you said, lines are where you act. A tinted band always means a message the person sent. The two lines at the bottom hold the live area: the input and any panel opened from it.
6. One edge, one grammar: rows share one left edge; the same kind of thing looks and reads the same on every screen.
7. Rare things live one step away: Settings and the version sit at the bottom.
